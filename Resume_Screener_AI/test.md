# Premium AI Resume Platform — Complete UI/UX Redesign Prompt

## 1. ROLE

Act as a **senior product designer, UX architect, SaaS UI designer, and frontend engineer** specializing in premium products such as Vercel, Linear, Notion, Stripe, Raycast, Framer, and modern AI SaaS platforms.

Redesign and improve my **complete resume-building / AI career platform**.

The uploaded reference images are visual inspiration only:

* Reference 1: premium dark SaaS analytics dashboard with charts, cards, metrics, sidebar navigation, and data visualization.
* Reference 2: premium modern SaaS landing page with dark background, strong typography, blue accents, responsive device mockups, and subtle futuristic visual effects.

Do **NOT copy the images literally**.

Instead, extract the design principles from them and create a completely original, professional, premium product experience specifically for an **AI Resume Builder / Resume Management / Career Analytics SaaS**.

The final result should look like a serious commercial SaaS product that could compete visually with premium products such as Linear, Vercel, Notion, Stripe, and modern AI productivity platforms.
